## The brief

Vertua is CEMEX's line of sustainable construction products. The goal was a website that generates leads for it, by showing construction projects that reduced their CO2 emissions with Vertua products.

## Two audiences

We started with user research: personas and user journeys. It showed two groups with different needs:

- **Creative users**: architects and designers, looking for inspiration.
- **Technical users**: civil and structural engineers and other construction professionals, looking for product details and tools.

The site had to serve both.

## Key decisions

### Two paths through one site

Creative users have an inspiration search. Technical users have product information and CEMEX's tools for engineers. Both paths connect to the same pieces: case studies, product information, content about Vertua, engineering tools, and an inspiration search.

### Case studies lead

The site showcases real construction projects that reduced CO2 emissions with Vertua products. They're the core of the lead generation goal.

### Inside CEMEX's brand

The design and color scheme follow CEMEX's existing style guidelines. The components were built in Figma, so the prototype could change quickly as feedback came in.

## Outcome

The deliverable was a functional prototype of the website, covering its pages and resources.

## What I'd measure

- **Leads by path**: how many leads come from creative users and how many from technical users.
- **Case studies**: how often a case study visit leads to a contact or a product page.
- **Engineering tools**: how many technical users use them before getting in touch.
