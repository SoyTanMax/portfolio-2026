## The brief

Persona Lamps needed a landing page for its NFT collection drop, where people mint NFTs straight into their wallets on Ethereum. The first version was made with a no-code website builder. My job was to redesign it and rebuild it in Vue.js.

The goals: a better-looking, easier page, and a smoother minting process.

## Design

The team provided strong art and graphics. I built the new design on top of them, so the page matched the collection's visual identity.

## Building it

I built the page in Vue.js. For minting, I connected it to the Ethereum network with ethers.js and Metamask, so people could go from their wallet to the smart contract without leaving the page.

## Outcome

All 4,444 NFTs sold during the drop. The collection later passed 700 ETH in sales volume on the secondary market.
